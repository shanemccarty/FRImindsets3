# FRI Semester 3 Peer Mentor Mindsets Guide

A Quarto book for First-year Research Immersion (FRI) peer mentors at Binghamton University,
supporting the FRI Semester 3 course across three research streams: Drug Discovery,
Environmental Visualization, and Public Health. One chapter per week: a mindset, a
self-assessment score against the cohort distribution, and a journal prompt.

**Live site:** <https://shanemccarty.github.io/FRImindsets3/>
