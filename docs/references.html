Measures & References

All measures are drawn from existing, validated instruments, with one documented exception: the Chapter 7 Feedback Orientation items are author-created for this course (no validated bipolar supportive/corrective scale exists). See the companion strategy document (FRI PM Mindsets Strategy) for the full item bank, scoring, and access status.

Not measured this semester

The pretest does not include Goal Preference, Facilitator, or Facilitator Reflection, so those mindsets are not part of this guide. The Five-Dimensional Curiosity Scale (Kashdan et al., 2020), the Mentor Self-Efficacy Scale, and the Paradox Mindset Scale (Miron-Spektor et al., 2018) are likewise parked. Their item banks are retained in the strategy document in case a chapter is reassigned.

How two scores are scaled
  • Feedback Orientation is collected on a 1–7 bipolar scale where 4 = Balanced. Chapter 7 subtracts 4 to re-centre it, so your reported score runs from −3 to +3 with 0 as balanced.
  • Leadership Role counts “evaluate the performance of the team” as a Task function, giving five Task and five Relational functions. Because the ten share a fixed 50-point budget, the two totals are complements of each other.
